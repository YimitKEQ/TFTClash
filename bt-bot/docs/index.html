BrosephTech Bot

Team guide - record meetings, turn them into tasks, see everything in one place

This bot sits in our Discord. It records a voice meeting, writes up a clean recap, turns the action items into tasks (on the board and in Jira), and shows the whole operation on a live dashboard. You mostly use two things: /record in a voice channel, and the dashboard in your browser.

Reading a card

Every command uses the same five status glyphs, so you learn them once.

🔴Overdue. The due date passed and the card is not finished.
🟠Gone quiet. Untouched in the same column for 5 days or more.
🟡Due soon. Landing within 2 days.
🟢Healthy. Moving, on time.
⛔Blocked. Cannot move until somebody clears it. Always sorts first.

The colour strip down the left of a card means the same thing: red needs you today, amber is a warning, green is fine. Dates render in your timezone, so "in 2 days" means two days for you.

1. Record a meeting

The headline feature. Hop in a voice channel, run two commands, and the bot does the rest.

  1. Join a voice channel and type /record start. The bot joins and starts listening. Everyone who talks is recorded on their own track, so it never cuts off after a pause, no matter how long the call runs.
  2. Have your meeting. Talk normally. Type /record status any time to see who it has heard.
  3. Type /record stop when you are done (you can add a title: /record stop title:Weekly sync). It transcribes the call, then writes the recap with AI. A long meeting takes a couple of minutes.
  4. Pick the real tasks. The bot shows a checklist of suggested tasks. Tick the ones that matter and hit Create selected. They become board cards and Jira issues, and the full recap posts to #bt-meetings.
Where your audio goes. Transcription runs on a hosted speech to text API, so the recorded audio is uploaded off our machine to be turned into text. It is deleted straight after, and only the text recap and transcript are kept. Our server is too small to do this locally: a forty minute call would take about six hours. If you would rather a conversation was not uploaded, use /meeting with pasted notes instead, which sends no audio at all.

2. What you get back

Instead of a wall of text, you get a structured recap you can actually skim. It lands in two places: a card in #bt-meetings (the permanent record) and the top of the dashboard.

📌
TL;DR - the one line that says what the meeting actually settled.
✅
Decisions - the firm calls the team committed to.
📋
Tasks created - the action items, each with a department, owner, and priority. These are the cards and Jira issues you approved.
🚧
Blockers - what is stuck or waiting, called out in red so it does not get lost.
📎
Full transcript - attached to the #bt-meetings post as a file if you ever need the exact words.

3. The dashboard

A live, read-only view of everything. It refreshes itself every 20 seconds. Lodie shares the link (it looks like https://<something>.trycloudflare.com/?k=YOUR_TOKEN). Open it once and it remembers you.

Do next

The single most useful panel: one ranked list of what needs action right now - overdue, blocked, and due-today work, top to bottom. Start here.

Latest meeting

The most recent recap, front and center: TL;DR, decisions, the tasks it created, blockers, and who was in the call. The full history sits in Meeting history lower down, where you can expand any past recap.

Jira board

A live kanban of our Jira (the KAN project): To Do, In Progress, Done, with each card colored by type and tagged with priority and owner. Click any card to open it in Jira.

The rest

KPIs - active, overdue, stuck, due soon, blocked, shipped, ideas Workload - active load per department Board pipeline - Ideas to Published Crew - each person's load Needs attention - overdue / due soon / stuck / blocked
The dashboard is for seeing. To do things (create a task, log metrics), use the Discord commands below. Open the dashboard on your phone too - it works.

4. Commands

Type these in any channel in our Discord.

/guide
This guide, inside Discord. Add topic: to jump to one part, or share:true to post it for everyone.
/record start · stop · status
Record the voice channel you are in and turn it into a recap plus tasks. jiracheck tests the Jira link.
/dashboard
A four part live report: the verdict and counters, Do next, where the work sits, and context (latest meeting, Jira). Has a Refresh button.
/meeting
Same as a recording but from pasted notes instead of audio. Good for async or a meeting the bot missed.
/card add · done · move
Create a card, ship it, or push it to another column. Start typing the title and autocomplete finds it.
/card block · unblock · assign
Flag what a card is waiting on, clear it, or hand it to someone. The reason is kept as a comment on the card.
/mytasks · /scorecard
Your own work, opening with the one card to start on. Or a crew member's accountability standing.
/board · /blocked
The pipeline as a kanban, and everything blocked that still needs unblocking.
/metrics log · show
Log YouTube / TikTok / Patreon numbers over time, with a trend sparkline.
/standup · /digest
Post the daily standup or the weekly digest right now.

6. FAQ

The bot says "Transcribing..." for a while. Is it stuck?
No. A long meeting takes a couple of minutes to transcribe, then a few more seconds to summarize. If it has been more than 5 minutes, ping Lodie.
I did a meeting but got no tasks.
If the conversation had no clear action items, it still posts the recap to #bt-meetings with no tasks. If summaries look basic, the AI key may be missing - tell Lodie.
The recap looks cut off in Discord.
The embed shows the highlights. The complete transcript is attached to the #bt-meetings post as a file.
The dashboard asks for a token.
Open the full link Lodie shared (it ends in ?k=...). After the first visit it remembers you.
Can I change a task or mark it done from the dashboard?
The web dashboard is read-only, but you no longer need it: /card done, /card move, /card block, /card unblock and /card assign all work straight from Discord, with autocomplete on the card title.
A list says "and 4 more". Where is the rest?
Discord caps how much fits in one card. The bot always shows whole rows and tells you how many it dropped, rather than cutting a name in half. Open the web dashboard for the full list.
Does the bot listen to us all the time?
No. It only records between /record start and /record stop, and the audio is processed on our machine and deleted right after.
